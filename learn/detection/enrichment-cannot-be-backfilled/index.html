Enrichment happens once, at ingest

Fields added as data arrives are frozen into the record. Add a new enrichment today and yesterday's events still will not carry it.

You add asset owner to your pipeline on Tuesday. Every event from Tuesday onward carries it. Every event before Tuesday does not, and never will.

That is what enrichment at ingest means. The lookup runs once, as the record is written, and the answer is baked in. Queries are fast later precisely because nothing has to be worked out at search time.

The trade shows up in two places.

History does not gain the new field. When an investigation spans the boundary, half your timeline has the owner and half does not, and the half that does not is usually the older half you needed.

And the stored value is the answer that was true then. An address enriched as benign in March stays benign in the record after the same address turns up on a blocklist in September. For forensics that is often what you want, because you are reconstructing what was known at the time. It is wrong if you assumed the field was current.

Query-time enrichment inverts both. History gains the new field, values are always current, and the lookup source has to still exist when you search.

Pick one enriched field. Find out whether it was written at ingest or resolved at query, then look at what your oldest retained events hold in it.

CHECK YOURSELF

Your team adds a new threat-intelligence enrichment on October 1. On October 20 you investigate activity from September. What do you expect to find in the September events?

SHOW THE ANSWER

No enrichment field at all, because it did not exist when those events were written

Why. Ingest-time enrichment writes the field as the record is created, so a record created before the enrichment existed has nothing to write. Applying it retroactively means reprocessing stored data, which is a deliberate and usually expensive operation rather than something that happens on its own. Re-indexing does sometimes re-run enrichment and is the closest wrong answer, but nothing in the scenario says anything was re-indexed. Scoring confidence by record age is not something mainstream platforms do.
