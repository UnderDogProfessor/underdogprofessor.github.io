COLD CHAIN IR

COLD CHAIN is a set of hands-on incident response labs I am building in public. Every lab takes place at the same made-up company, Rimeport Cold Logistics, which stores and delivers chilled and frozen food.

Level 1 is the basics: one Windows computer, one small thing to find, about an hour each. The lab machine is a Windows 11 Enterprise virtual machine in VMware Workstation named WS-DSP-01, the computer of Dana, a dispatcher at the North Yard warehouse.
