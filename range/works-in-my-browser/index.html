Before you start: how this works
01One objective at a timeYou are always told, in one sentence, what you are trying to establish right now. Only the actions that serve it are shown, and you can open the full list whenever you like.
02Time passesEvery action costs minutes in the story. Nothing is on a real-world timer, so think as long as you like, but the minutes inside the incident have consequences in it.
03Some things cannot be undoneWrong moves make the incident bigger, not just lower-scoring. This one can reach BUSINESS IMPACT, where the run ends and somebody more senior takes it off you.
04Mistakes are the lessonEvery wrong turn is written up at the end with what it cost and what the better move was. Finish the range (any outcome) and its interview pack unlocks.

You are judged on three things. Accuracy: did you work out what was really wrong and deal with it. Economy: how directly you got there, in the incident’s own minutes. Integrity: what you preserved, who you told, and whether you claimed anything you had not established. The third is the one people fail.

Finish it, with any outcome, and its interview pack unlocks: 4 questions with weak, good and strong answers, the follow-up, and the trap.
