The
Range

Every range is built around a scenario that comes up in security interviews. It arrives the way real tickets arrive: wrong, vague, and already diagnosed by somebody who guessed. You investigate, you decide, and the incident reacts. Wrong moves do not deduct points. They make it bigger, and some of them cannot be undone.

16 ranges10 of 10 domains4 levels57 interview questions

New to this? Start with Meeting Room B. Recruit ranges guide you one objective at a time, mark the best first step, and keep the clock gentle. Each level after that takes some of the help away.

Every range starts as a service fault or a breach. Some faults turn out to be breaches partway through. What each range teaches is shown once you have finished it, because naming it first would give the answer away.
